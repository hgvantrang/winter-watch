# Winter service plan

## Section 3 — Ground temperature limits
Priority 1 routes: grit when forecast ground temperature reaches 1.0°C or below.
Priority 2 routes: grit when forecast ground temperature reaches 0.5°C or below.
Priority 3 routes: grit when forecast ground temperature reaches -1.0°C or below.

## Section 4 — Wet-road test
Treat a route as potentially wet when there is precipitation in the forecast or the dew point is within 1°C of the ground temperature.

## Section 5 — Depot rule
For Priority 3 routes, check that the depot has at least 40% of its stated salt capacity before dispatch. If capacity is unavailable, flag the route for an engineer check.

Departure timing: finish gritting one hour before the ground reaches 0°C, allow one hour per 20 km of route length, and never schedule departure before 17:00.
